RIMURU COMPANION

Copy this entire 0100_rimuru folder into a VPet mod folder.
Enable it in Settings > Mods, select Rimuru, and select English. Restart VPet.

Includes 25 pose families, contextual idle actions, and distinct start/loop/end phases,
eating, drinking, talking, reading, carry, rest, stretch and celebration loops.
Idle actions respond to energy, mood, time of day and interaction, with cooldowns.
Eating and drinking show a bread roll and mug for all corresponding items.
No code plugins, account credentials, or paid services are required.
